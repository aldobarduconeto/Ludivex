## Prompt

Design one refined, professional app launcher icon for GameHub, a videogame price tracking app. Square opaque PNG, full-bleed solid midnight navy #0B1116 background, no outer border or rounded-square frame. A single bold geometric monogram G in fresh lime green #B8F75A, subtly incorporating a price-tag angled cut into the lower-right stroke and one tiny dark circular tag hole. The G should feel like a confident modern gaming and savings brand, compact and highly recognizable at 24px. Flat vector-like geometry, precise clean edges, consistent thick strokes, balanced negative space. Center the mark within the central 60 percent of the canvas with generous safe padding. Absolutely no game controller, no d-pad, no extra text, no numbers, no 3D, no gradients, no glow, no shadows, no mockup. One finished icon only.
